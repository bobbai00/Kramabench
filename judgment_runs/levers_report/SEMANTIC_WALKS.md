# Semantic walks — every C1/C2/C3 accuracy diff, gold-plan-aligned

Per-step semantic walks (compare-traces skill, accuracy procedure) for all
**27 exclusive-win pairs / 15 unique tasks** across C1 (3k vs 5k), C2
(schema-only vs stats-D2), C3 (Delta vs Latest). Method per task: extract
the gold solution's semantic plan (`solutions/<domain>/<task>.py`), walk
each arm's `react_steps.json` action-by-action (code, not prose;
`scripts/extract_walk.py`), pin the FIRST semantic divergence per arm,
quote the rendered evidence at that step, verdict per the accept rules.
Individual walks: `semantic_walks/<task>.md`. Several agents *mechanically
reproduced* the losers' exact wrong answers from their divergent semantics.

## What the walks changed (corrections to prior reports)

1. **The C3 "thrash blind spot" needs rewording** (REPORT.md §6): on BOTH
   churn exhibits (astronomy-hard-9, wildfire-hard-17) every re-probe was
   created with its earlier refutation FULLY rendered — Latest never
   dropped the evidence, and astronomy-hard-9's contexts even carried
   explicit engine "do NOT resubmit a near-variant" reflections. Latest
   churn is a **behavioral mode difference (mint-new-op vs edit-in-place),
   not evidence starvation.** It remains real and cost-relevant (20 dead
   sinks, 4× cost, step-cap death) — but it is not an information-delivery
   failure the history lever "fixes"; it is an interaction pattern the
   Delta framing suppresses.
2. **Both "triple convergence" candidates dissolved** (environment-hard-7:
   ×100 unit coin on bit-identical values; environment-hard-8: keep-first
   vs melt-all with the signal rendered to the loser). Multi-ray agreement
   against one arm is NOT evidence of lever causation when the majority
   convention explains the agreement.
3. **legal-hard-15 upgraded**: now attributed with exact mechanical repro
   and step-level render quotes on both winning sides. It remains the only
   accuracy flip the render levers own.

## Failure-mechanism taxonomy (what actually breaks, 15 tasks)

- **Convention/interpretation coin-flips** (7): unit ×100, period formula,
  join-key grain, keep-first station, count+1 header, correlation
  threshold, L2-vs-L∞ metric. Rendered evidence identical; the model's
  prior decides. No render lever can carry this information.
- **Silent parse corruption** (3): bare `read_fwf` DOY truncation,
  concat date mis-alignment, densest-column heuristic hitting Rain.
  Winners on these tasks won by *failing loudly* (0-row outputs forcing
  re-parses). Lever implication: this is an ENGINE/observability lever
  (parse sanity signals — e.g., render "column X: 100% single-digit
  values" style anomalies), not a sampling/stats/history lever.
- **Hidden-in-data facts** (2): NWS-ID whose rendered sample was NaN
  (row-0 trap), ASCII-`--`-vs-en-dash key mismatch. Invisible at ANY cap
  in the current renders; would need value-distribution or key-overlap
  probes (join telemetry) to surface.
- **Aggregation-grain blindness under fused pipelines** (the attributed
  one): dedup evidence exists in the DATA and surfaces via 5k rows OR the
  dup-profile line — but only if an intermediate is materialized rather
  than fused into a 1×1. The render lever works exactly where the agent's
  own DAG structure lets it.

## Implication for the paper

The three context levers are **cost/behavior levers with one narrow,
well-understood accuracy channel** (duplicate/grain evidence on
materialized intermediates). The recurring accuracy killers on KramaBench
are convention priors and silent parse corruption — both live OUTSIDE the
render-parameter space, in engine observability (loud failures, anomaly
lines, join/key telemetry) and answer-convention handling. That redirects
the "accuracy" chapter toward observability levers, and squarely supports
positioning sampling/stats/history as the cost-efficiency levers the
levers report measured.
