After controlling for weather conditions (humidity, wind, ERC, rain) and other covariates (region, year, cause), the NOAA data do **not** support the idea that more aggressive suppression (Full Suppression vs Other) makes fires end faster: Full Suppression is associated with a **longer** time to control (about **+20.5 days**, statistically significant), and it is also associated with **more** buildings threatened (about **+418** buildings, statistically significant), rather than fewer.
