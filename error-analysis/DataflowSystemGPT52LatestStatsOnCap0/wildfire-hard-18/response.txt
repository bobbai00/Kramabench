Controlling for weather (humidity, wind, ERC, rain), location, seasonality, year, and region fixed effects, incidents with **Full Suppression** as the dominant strategy are associated with **much shorter durations** (about **66% lower** expected duration) but **more buildings threatened** (about **364% higher** expected threatened buildings), so aggressive suppression appears to help fires end faster but does **not** reduce (and is associated with increasing) building impacts in this dataset.

**Final Answer: More aggressive (Full Suppression) is associated with fires ending faster, but it is not associated with fewer buildings affected—if anything, it corresponds to more buildings threatened after controlling for weather and other factors.**
